# Suggested Instrumental Rationale

## Overall approach

- I used this bucket for the strongest process, arrangement, and sound-design material that did not quite belong in the personal journal lane or the main Instagram lane.
- The goal here is not to repeat the emotional testimony from the other files, but to give the instrumental rollout its own reason to exist.
- These drafts lean on groove, tone, pacing, layering, and recording method so the posts feel native to instrumental releases rather than like caption leftovers.

## Joint Queen

- Rehomed the "groove first" language here because it works better as a sonic thesis than as a personal life-story beat.
- Left the joy framing in place, but tied it to space, bass movement, and guitar detail.
- This became the right place for the architecture of the track rather than the biography behind it.

## Stash Daddy

- Focused on the loop-and-response recording method because that is the most compelling craft angle in the source material.
- Avoided repeating the emotional liberation story from the main caption and instead centered discovery in the playing itself.
- This makes the post useful for listeners who respond to process as much as personality.

## Space Cruiser

- Used the instrumental bucket to foreground collage editing, delay work, and atmosphere-building.
- Held back the broader life context, which belongs more naturally to the main Instagram and journal versions.
- Kept the language airy and cinematic so the post matches the track's actual sonic behavior.

## Home

- The instrumental post now has a clear identity: arrangement as healing.

## Decay

- This lets the instrumental post speak directly to the sound of the track.

## Resolve

## Poetry

## Gratitude
