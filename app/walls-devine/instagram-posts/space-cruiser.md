## Current Draft
I had just played one of the worst shows of my life. The kind that makes you wonder if you even like the process of attempting to make and do music at all. It wasn’t that long ago - September of 2025. I was 36 and had been playing music live for 22 years.

Depressed about the whole shebang, I went to a friend's house for solace and they put on “Screaming Masterpiece”, a documentary about Icelandic music. It was amazing, and timely, to see some of the sonic influences I had idolized growing up explaining their worldviews and take on, and approach to music.

One of the main aspects I appreciated from that film and the people featured within it, was that they basically decided: “There’s no point to trying to do music here to be famous, whatever. It’s an island of 400,000 people. So we just make the music we want to hear.”

I woke up the morning after the show and started writing an unnamed song, simply to get the taste out of my mouth from the show and to make something that I would enjoy as a listener. It was really the first song I wrote for the record, not even knowing that was what I was doing. I actually forgot about it for about five months, until the creation of Joint Queen and Stash Daddy were begging for an ending to form a sort of trilogy of songs that are thematically based upon a screenplay I’d written called Bong Tour.

Our friend @dustinstonebrook heard the initial mix and simply responded: “If you’re calling it Space Cruiser… it needs to be spacier.”

That was the start of forming the sort of musical template for Volume 1, which involved collage editing together lots of takes and ideas with delays to form things we could have never thought of had we sat to write a song the traditional way.

After smoking weed with Dustin, Terry and I linked up with @ishanthakur, an amazing singer and musician who I know from my travels through India. Ishan added a mesmerizing sargam part and really made the song have more of a transcendent and world music feel than any of the other tracks on the record.

One night, while the mic was rolling, Terry added a bong rip to the track, and that’s when I knew the song was complete. I don’t make up the rules, I just smoke weed.

Some people sample drums. Some people sample grief. Some people, with reckless abandon, sample the atmosphere.

We hope you enjoy the ride. Drive safe, my friends.

John Walls
