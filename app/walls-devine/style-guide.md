# Walls Devine Style Guide

## Intent

Walls Devine should read like a boutique product unveiling for an album world. The page is not a utility dashboard. It is a staged reveal of the object, the collector grid, and the listening experience around it.

## Palette

- Primary ink: deep charcoal / thunder black
- Primary accent: oxblood red
- Surface: warm paper cream and soft parchment pink
- Supporting effects: subtle silver smoke, border engraving, restrained lightning seams

## Typography

- Display copy: elegant serif treatment with generous scale and calm pacing
- Metadata and UI labels: compact uppercase mono or condensed sans
- Body copy: direct editorial prose, not marketing sludge

## Layout Modules

- Hero: product-unveiling moment with the cover framed like edition zero
- Experience framing: short editorial cards that explain why the page exists
- Collector grid: nine tiles as one installation, with the center cover acting as the anchor
- Listening room: modular player, song cards, and journal links collected in one place
- Song notes: making and technical details stay tucked inside expandable track details

## Interaction Rules

- Buttons should feel like quiet premium controls, not loud app CTAs
- Expandable notes should stay secondary and tucked away
- Cross-links into Bong Tour should appear as companion pathways, not promotional banners

## Imagery Rules

- Every gradient, border, and accent should trace back to the cover art and supporting tiles
- Use light texture, print-like framing, and controlled shadows to imply a physical object
- Avoid glossy tech-product chrome or overly dark nightclub aesthetics

## Responsive Behavior

- Mobile should preserve the sense of sequence: hero, framing, gallery, listening room
- The center cover should remain visually special even when the grid collapses
- Cross-links and CTA clusters should wrap cleanly without feeling cramped
