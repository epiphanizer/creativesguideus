# Walls Devine Agent Notes

- Scope: This route owns the Walls Devine album-unveiling experience, including [app/walls-devine/page.tsx](app/walls-devine/page.tsx), [components/walls-devine/WallsDevineLanding.tsx](components/walls-devine/WallsDevineLanding.tsx), [styles/sections/_walls-devine.scss](styles/sections/_walls-devine.scss), [components/walls-devine/content.ts](components/walls-devine/content.ts), [app/walls-devine/instagram-posts](app/walls-devine/instagram-posts), and [public/walls-devine/journals](public/walls-devine/journals).
- Page role: Treat Walls Devine as a boutique album product reveal. The page should feel collectible, editorial, and calm under pressure rather than like a standard content hub.
- Visual rule: Pull palette and ornament from the artwork first. Favor oxblood, aged paper, charcoal, engraved borders, and soft lightning/smoke motifs over neutral portfolio styling.
- Typography rule: Lead with refined display treatment for titles and restrained mono or compact sans for metadata. Avoid generic SaaS typography rhythms.
- Content rule: Keep the hero, collector grid, edition specs, and campaign copy system legible as distinct modules in a premium narrative sequence.
- Cross-link rule: Bong Tour links should stay visible and deliberate, especially around score cues, but they should never overpower the album object itself.
- Asset rule: Walls Devine imagery now lives in [app/walls-devine/assets](app/walls-devine/assets). Keep future artwork organized by purpose, not in the app root.
- Edit rule: Preserve the separation between public-facing journals in [public/walls-devine/journals](public/walls-devine/journals) and admin/source drafts in [app/walls-devine/instagram-posts](app/walls-devine/instagram-posts).
- Avoid: Flat white sections, purple accents, generic service-site cards, over-explaining the release strategy in the hero, or introducing visual language that does not trace back to the cover art.
