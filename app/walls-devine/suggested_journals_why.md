# Suggested Journals Rationale

## Overall approach

- I treated the journal suggestions as the place to preserve the richer autobiographical and spiritual material that was too long or too layered for Instagram.
- I merged the best personal passages from the original and edited drafts into cleaner longform profiles for each song.
- I removed release-date footers, "all platforms" language, and repeated CTA language entirely.
- I held back the most process-heavy and arrangement-heavy leftovers for a separate instrumental-post bucket, so the journals could stay focused on personal meaning.
- I kept author voice first, but shaped each entry around a single emotional thesis so the journals read like coherent profiles rather than transcript dumps.

## Joint Queen

- Restored more of Terry's deeper creative-turning-point language from the original, especially the realization that the music had not yet become Us.
- Left out the very long gear and muse digressions, but kept the life-change and joy-discovery stakes.
- Framed the song as the opening thesis of the record rather than a standard making-of note.

## Stash Daddy

- Expanded the interior transformation around recording without rehearsal, because that is the real story of the song.
- Kept the kitchen-table beginning as a grounding image, but did not let it dominate the piece.
- Preserved John's key lyric line because it carries the emotional logic of the entry.

## Space Cruiser

- Brought back the worst-show context and the Iceland documentary influence because those details genuinely explain why the song exists.
- Kept the Bong Tour trilogy connection and the collage-process insight because they help the piece function as a profile, not just a caption.
- Cut the longer joke material once it stopped deepening the theme of doubt turning into myth.

## Home

- Restored the fuller autobiographical context around Los Angeles, return, and nervous-system reset.
- Condensed the more casual side anecdotes so the piece stays centered on recovery, receptivity, and return.
- Kept the later production story because it shows how an older fragment became a healing document.

## Decay

- Elevated the trust and intuition theme, which is the heart of Terry's original copy.
- Preserved the spoken-word line because it is the clearest spiritual turning point in the piece.
- Reduced repetition so the journal feels like a sharpened testimony rather than an unfiltered monologue.

## Resolve

- Kept the essay-like voice because it suits the song, but tightened the logic around crucible, congruence, and self-contract.
- Removed the promo footer and let the philosophical arc stand on its own.
- Treated the song as a threshold document rather than a campaign asset.

## Poetry

- Preserved the breakup origin, the Marianne Moore thread, and the movement from singular authorship into shared humanity.
- Moved away from the more sprawling literary digression and toward the idea of life fighting its way through the song.
- Kept the journal version reflective and essay-like because that voice is native to the source material.

## Gratitude

- Kept the closer realization and the recognition that something once imagined had become real.
- Preserved the peace-and-anchor language because it is the emotional gift of the song.
- Reduced the thank-you section so the profile stays centered on transformation rather than acknowledgements.
