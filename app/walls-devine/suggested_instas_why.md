# Suggested Instagram Rationale

## Overall approach

- I treated Instagram as the place for one strong emotional thesis per song, not the full autobiography.
- I cut repeated release footers, "link in bio" language, and most hard-sell phrasing so the captions read like testimony rather than rollout copy.
- I kept the voice of the original writer on each song and preserved the most quotable, personal turns.
- I held back a separate set of more sonic, arrangement-driven details for instrumental-release posts, since that material crowded the emotional center of the main captions.
- I moved secondary anecdotes, production tangents, and wider life context into the journal suggestions whenever they slowed the caption or weakened the central feeling.

## Joint Queen

- Kept the core awakening arc: "we weren't at the heart of it" -> John's tracks arrive -> joy becomes the thesis.
- Cut the long prior-project setup, the Mint-Green P-Bass sidebar, and the extended muse/love material because they diluted the main emotional breakthrough.
- Ended on joy instead of promo so the caption feels lived, not launched.

## Stash Daddy

- Kept the kitchen-table origin and the revelation that recording could happen without rehearsal or fear.
- Centered the "heart being set free" line because it is the clearest emotional payoff in the draft.
- Cut repetition in the studio explanation and left out extra staging details that did not deepen the feeling.

## Space Cruiser

- Kept the worst-show origin, the Iceland documentary trigger, the Bong Tour trilogy connection, and the collage-production method.
- Preserved the atmospheric humor of the bong-rip completion because it feels specific and human.
- Cut the longer derivative/Sublime detour and the Rishikesh aside because they pulled attention away from the song's inner logic.

## Home

- Kept the Los Angeles collapse, the empty-house reset, and the eventual gathering of family and healing into the finished track.
- Cut the fence joke and some of the extra setup so the caption lands faster on the emotional center.
- Let the post close on hope rather than release info, because the song's power is in the return, not the announcement.

## Decay

- Kept the "metal song" remark, the trust revelation, and the death-expressed-as-life line as the emotional spine.
- Removed repetition around validation and condensed the narrative so the transformation feels sharper.
- Preserved the most spiritually charged language because that is what separates the post from generic gear talk.

## Resolve

- This draft was already cleaner than most, so the edit mostly tightened it into a single statement of becoming.
- Kept the crucible, self-contract, and ash imagery because those are the defining motifs.
- Cut the release footer and let the final blessing do the closing work.

## Poetry

- Kept the problem of earning the title, the Marianne Moore reference, and the movement from private heartbreak into shared humanity.
- Cut the longer D.H. Lawrence digression for Instagram; it is better suited to journal-length writing.
- Focused the caption on genuineness and mess, which are the most resonant ideas in the source material.

## Gratitude

- Kept the album-closer realization, the sense that something had become real, and the peace-through-gratitude arc.
- Cut repetition around gratitude as a concept and removed the explicit thank-you list from the social version.
- Ended in the emotional truth of the song rather than the campaign frame.
