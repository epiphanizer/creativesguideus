# Work Module Style Guide

## Role

The work module is a separate proof surface, not the emotional front door of the CGU site. It should feel cleaner, more editorial, and more direct than the music and screenplay worlds.

## Content Hierarchy

- Lead with concise proof and partner clarity.
- Keep summary cards short enough to syndicate into other properties.
- Preserve outbound links to live sites and links back to full case studies in `cgu_master`.

## Sharing Contract

- Source JSON: [data/work/module.json](data/work/module.json)
- Export command: `npm run sync:work-module`
- Generated target: `seanhalls_online/src/data/cgu-work-module.json`

## Tone

- Precise, legible, and measured.
- More proof-oriented than the album or screenplay pages, but never generic agency-speak.
- Keep technical detail in the case studies; keep the shared module concise.
