# Bong Tour Style Guide

## Intent

Bong Tour should feel like walking into a branded portal for a film myth. The page must sell tone, not just summarize plot. It should read like a premium pitch campaign with a living soundtrack bridge to Walls Devine.

## Palette

- Base surfaces: warm parchment, toasted paper, and sand-toned light
- Accent one: saffron / ember orange
- Accent two: smoke green
- Accent three: occasional dusk violet or motel-night shadow inside music cards

## Typography

- Headlines: bold cinematic display treatment with strong uppercase rhythm
- Supporting metadata: mono or condensed utility voice
- Body copy: textured but direct; sharp enough to feel pitch-ready

## Layout Modules

- Hero: poster artifact, logline, quick facts, and signal cards
- Experience bridge: explains the page system and the soundtrack relationship
- Score posters: modular cue cards that deepen the world and cross-link to Walls Devine
- Tone, cast, storyboard, themes, packaging, finale: each should feel like its own pitch panel within one cohesive portal

## Interaction Rules

- Cross-links to Walls Devine should be easy to spot from the hero, score section, and finale
- Buttons should feel like premium tags or invitations, not default app controls
- Section cards can be expressive, but the reading path must stay clear on mobile

## Surface Treatment

- Use film-grain, smoke glow, light poster warping, and layered gradients sparingly but intentionally
- Treat cue posters as collectible panels with distinct palettes per card
- Avoid flat white cards unless they are deliberately framed by warmer atmospheric layers

## Responsive Behavior

- Mobile should still feel like a cinematic sequence, not stacked leftovers
- Keep the poster, signal cards, and score posters near the top of the reading experience
- Preserve cross-link buttons without forcing awkward overflow or truncated labels
