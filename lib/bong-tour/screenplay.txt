ACT 1
EXT. NEAR THE GANGES RIVER, WEST BENGAL, INDIA - AFTERNOON
Along the banks of the sacred Ganges river in West Bengal, we
see a badly maimed and bloodied little person, MONTU, with an
acid-scarred face, cradling what looks like a baby in his
arms. Explosions are heard in the distance. sweat rolls down
his dirty face. He finds a small basket, and carefully sets
the thing most precious to him down. Going through remarkable
physical pain and strain to do so, he uses his last strength
to push the basket down the river, drawing allegory to Moses.
A tear trickles down his face as he collapses along the
shore.
The basket is dragged down the river, until it slowly starts
to sink, unravelling the cloth and revealing THE BONG, which
is slowly filled with the refuse of the sacred Ganges.
As the Bong disappears underwater, bubbles begin to fill the
basin.
