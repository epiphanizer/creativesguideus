# Decay — Studio Journal

Date window: Volume 1 sessions

## Intent
Decay is meant to sound like memory collapsing and reforming at the same time.

## Making Notes
- Built from fragments of earlier drafts to preserve history in the arrangement.
- Leaned into asymmetry and unstable transitions.
- Preserved imperfections that contributed to emotional texture.

## Technical Notes
- Guitar harmonics were resampled and granulated.
- Top-end was intentionally dulled in spots for weathered character.
- Automation drives slow-motion tension between sections.

## Visual Tie-In
Overgrowth and cracked-object iconography reflects the song's ruin-and-revival posture.
