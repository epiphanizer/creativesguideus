# Conviction — Studio Journal

**By John Walls & Terry Devine**

Conviction is the midpoint pivot of Volume 1. Moving between the cosmic delay beds of Space Cruiser and the full-throttle screams of Decay, it grounds the record in live rhythm and deliberate forward motion.

Tracked with tube saturation, heavy bass, and driving drum energy, it holds the line at the center of the album.

— Walls/Devine
