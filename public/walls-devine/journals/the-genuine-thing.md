# The Genuine Thing — Studio Journal

**By John Walls & Terry Devine**

### What We Do for the Audience

When Terry and I sat down to record the tracks that became *Volume 1*, we didn't start with a marketing plan or an algorithm. We started with two chairs in a living room, a vintage Mint-Green P-Bass bought from a friend, and a simple decision: to stop apologizing for our lives.

For decades, artists are told to perform. You build a persona, you smooth the edges, you compress the dynamics, and you deliver a product designed to keep people casually entertained while they scroll. But if you’ve ever sat alone in a dark room with headphones on, heartbroken or exhausted or searching for something real, you know that performance isn't enough. You don't want a performance. You want communion.

That is what we do for the audience: **We meet you inside the room.**

> “I too, dislike it… but, there is in it after all, a place for the genuine.”  
> — Marianne Moore, *Poetry*

### The Anatomy of Truth

“The Genuine Thing” is our thesis statement. It gathers everything that happened to us across the eight tracks of *Volume 1* and puts it right into your ears.

1. **The Scratches**: It begins with the sound of Terry’s pick scraping muted strings and the subtle hum of a cranked tube amp before the first note drops. Those aren’t mistakes left on tape; they are the physical proof of human hands touching wood and steel.
2. **The Pocket**: John’s Mint-Green P-Bass drives the groove at 92 BPM with a syncopated, walking pulse. It grounds you. It says: *no matter how chaotic your mind is, the heart still keeps time.*
3. **The Harmonium & Atmospheric Delay**: The cosmic bed underneath the guitars isn’t synthetic filler — it’s a living reed harmonium breathing with slow bellows, collaged through analog tape delays to remind you of the infinite space waiting just behind the everyday noise.
4. **The Screaming Guitars**: When Terry plugs into the Mesa Mark and lets the guitar wail in the middle of the song, that is the sound of grief and survival turning into defiance. *Death was contained in us, expressed as life.*
5. **The Spoken Word**: We don't hide behind metaphor when the truth needs saying. *You are poetry. You are fiercity. Write your life. Share your humanity.*
6. **The Exhale**: And when the distortion clears, we leave you with warmth, a gentle acoustic resolve, and a quiet whisper of gratitude.

### A Sanctuary for the Messy

I hope this song hits you when you are messy. When the ink has spilled across your desk, when the relationship ended, when the venture collapsed, or when you finally realized that the mask you’ve been wearing for twenty years is suffocating you.

Step into the listening room. Turn the lights down. Listen all the way through. 

You don’t have to prove anything here. The music was always inside you.

— John Walls & Terry Devine
